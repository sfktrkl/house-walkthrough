Features & keyboard shortcuts

Views and camera

  • P floor-plan editing view; V first-person walkthrough.
  • In plan view: right-drag to rotate the camera, middle-drag to pan, and use the wheel to zoom.
  • In walk view: WASD to move, mouse to look, Esc to release the mouse.

Objects and construction

  • Spawn furniture, appliances, basic shapes, and modular walls from the left catalog.
  • The floor is not selectable. Left-click toggles an object in or out of the selection. The Selected objects list has an × button for each item; Esc clears all.
  • Left-drag any selected object to move the whole selection. R rotates all selected objects 90° around their shared center; arrows nudge all by 10 cm.
  • For basic shapes, E sets elevation above the floor and K sets a uniform or X/Y/Z scale; Y is height.
  • Delete or Backspace removes the selected object. Movable walls affect walkthrough collision.
  • Combine basic shapes and modular walls to create custom forms and rooms on the continuous ground plane.

Labels and measurements

  • Spawn, move, rotate, and delete custom text labels. T edits one selected text or measurement label.
  • M starts room measurement; drag a rectangle to calculate m² with 10 cm snapping. The live total sums all current measurements.
  • A finished measurement shows only its non-blocking text label. Select one and press T to rename only its room name; dimensions and area stay unchanged.
  • Esc cancels an unfinished measurement.

Saving

  • Changes are autosaved locally, including spawned/deleted objects, labels, measurements, and positions.
  • Ctrl+S exports a JSON layout; Ctrl+O imports one.
  • 0 restores the original house and removes spawned objects.
  • H opens or closes this complete feature guide.
